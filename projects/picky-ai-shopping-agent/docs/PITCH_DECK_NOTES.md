# Picky — Pitch Deck Notes (12 Slides)

**Source:** Team SSKL's 12-page Picky pitch presentation, dated 2026.

> This Markdown document is a structured summary of the uploaded presentation, not the original PDF, working code or evidence that proposed functions have been built.

## 01. Title
**Picky** — 당신을 가장 잘 아는 AI 쇼핑 에이전트  
Personal Shopping Agent: 탐색부터 비교, 구매 결정까지.

## 02. Problem
Shopping for one product requires searching multiple platforms, reading and assessing many reviews, and calculating the real total price after options, delivery and discounts.

## 03. Solution
An AI agent that learns user preferences and consumption history to support three stages:
**탐색 → 비교 → 구매 의사결정**.

## 04. Product
Four proposed pillars:
1. **MEMORY:** Long-term record of tastes, sizes and purchase histories.
2. **ANALYSIS:** Cross-platform product, review and price analysis.
3. **RECOMMEND:** Personalized options with explanations.
4. **PROACTIVE:** Earlier suggestions for repeat purchases and price changes.

## 05. Why Now
The pitch connects rapid advances in LLM/AI Agent technology with consumer familiarity with AI-driven search and Q&A.

## 06. Market Size
A market framing around **global e-commerce → AI shopping assistants → personalized commerce**. The source slides present the categories, not quantified market-size figures.

## 07. Competition
Positioning: **검색하는 AI가 아닌, 나를 기억하는 AI** — distinguish a continuously personalized, proactive shopping experience from one-off search answers.

This is the team's *competitive thesis*, not a verified capability comparison with every version of another AI product.

## 08. Business Model
Freemium with three proposed tiers:
- **FREE:** ₩0, baseline product search.
- **INTELLIGENCE:** ₩9,900–14,900/month, memory and personalized recommendations.
- **AGENT:** ₩19,900–29,900/month, proactive recommendations and purchase assistance.

## 09. Team
The pitch identifies the team as **SSKL** and discusses four competencies: AI Agent, E-commerce, Data Analytics and Consumer Experience.

## 10. Go-to-Market
Three proposed steps:
1. Start with AI-native early adopters.
2. Validate in a specific shopping category.
3. Expand across categories using recommendation and purchasing outcomes.

## 11. Milestones
Proposed sequence: core agent development and early-user validation → mobile app launch target by **D+90** → AI-platform integration → multi-platform agent ecosystem.

These are targets, not completed milestones.

## 12. Closing
**“쇼핑의 다음은, 나를 아는 AI입니다.”**
